Our new design: ASTAIR

As we mentioned before we are working on the design for one of the apartments in Rem Koolhaas' 'De Rotterdam'. Towering over the city, looking all the way over to the harbor area; from this building you have an amazing view on Rotterdam.


Last week one of our newly developed designs was installed. We are proud to introduce you to: ASTAIR; our RGB and white custom made light tiles with heavy glass top. The way the light-tiles reflect in the windows and optically multiply outside the apartment - high above the ground - reminds us of the candles in the 'Galerie des Glaces' at Versailles. In a contemporary version of course...
