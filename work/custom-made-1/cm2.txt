Custom Made...

As independent light designers  you can basically work towards the best result for your customers. That's a fact. This result is mainly about light and not so much about design armatures, as we have mentioned before.

 

The way we look at light design leans more towards art and aesthetics, but also what's currently a better option for our environment. Needless to say: the latter changes about every month. 

 

We often combine high quality designer lamps with our own custom made light solutions. Executed in steel, wood, glas, stone, concrete and/or modern materials like acrylic stone and (HiMacs or Betacryl) and transparent acrylics. These products we design and develop together with our (Dutch) partners to deliver products that have perfect detailing and high quality materials. Over the last year we have designed several unique (integrated) light solutions you will not find anywhere else...
