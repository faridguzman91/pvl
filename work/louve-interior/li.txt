LOUVE interior

In the middle of 2012 lingerie store LOUVE opened it's doors in Rotterdam. Very soon after the opening, a few of the most popular glossy magazines in Holland (Marie Clair and LINDA) started to write about "probably the most beautiful lingerie store in The Netherlands.." Both the interior design and the light design was done by Paul van Laak. Although the LOUVE founders didn't make it, these photo's show the warm and magical atmosphere of the store.
