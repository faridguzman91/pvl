Continuation: Nieuwe Binnenweg

After the Middellandstraat, the owners and businesses of the Nieuwe Binnenweg, also joined in. Atelier 81 designed a similar but connected design by also using the 'initials' of the street (NB) for the armatures. Both shopping streets are close together and share a neighborhood. At the same time they both cherish their own identity. With this sequel-design, which was installed late 2021, we connected both shopping areas in Rotterdam West without disregarding this fact.
