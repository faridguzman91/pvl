Openbare verlichting — Rotterdam

Our Lady Virgin II

Deze tekst is alleen in het Engels beschikbaar.

The light design plan for this charming part of our Rotterdam city centre called the MaHo quarter was installed. CLS Led technology in Wijchen (NL). They are the supplier of the armatures for this specific task. The armatures are firm, have a steady light source suitable for outdoor and they give us the 12 degrees beam angle we were looking for. what we tried to achieve was to create small beams of light coming from high up in the crown of the trees, shining down on the street and creating a pattern of leaves on the pavement.

Last but not least: you might wonder where the Lady Virgen of Holland is? You can see the statue in front of the large edifice on the photo below. (photo: private collection)
